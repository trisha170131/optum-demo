Acute Care Workflow

This demo shows registration for emergency/walk-in patients where identity is unknown:

  • Patient Arrival: EMS or walk-in arrival with unknown identity
  • Identity Resolution: Staff gathers patient info via ambient listening or manual entry
  • EHR Lookup: System searches for patient in regional MPI
  • RTE Verification: If match found, check insurance eligibility
  • Registration Complete: Create record or merge with existing

8-Step Workflow

1️⃣ Arrival & Consent
2️⃣ Identity Capture (Ambient or Manual)
3️⃣ Auto-Fill from EHR
4️⃣ Real-Time Eligibility Check
5️⃣ Delta Capture (New/Changed Fields)
6️⃣ Staff Confirmation
7️⃣ Consent Signature
8️⃣ Copay Collection

Key Integrations

EHR Matching: Search multiple identifiers (name, DOB, insurance) against regional MPI and Epic

Ambient Listening: AI transcribes patient/staff conversation in real-time to extract demographics

RTE Verification: Instant insurance eligibility and copay lookup

Speech-to-Text: Convert ambient conversation to structured patient data

Scenarios

Scenario 1: High-confidence match - Patient known, auto-filled, RTE succeeds

Scenario 2: Partial match - Insurance changed mid-visit, needs manual correction

Scenario 3: Unknown patient - EMS arrival, trauma MRN, ambient listening

Scenario 4: Declines recording - Patient opts out, staff types manually

Real-Time Eligibility in Acute Care

RTE is critical for acute settings where:

  • Patients may not have current insurance information
  • Coverage eligibility must be verified before treatment
  • Copay collection happens at registration
  • Insurance changes are common (job loss, spouse coverage, etc.)
