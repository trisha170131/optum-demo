3. Diane Foster — Full Cascade

Auto-resolution flags items, SMS misses, escalates to kiosk, then front desk

Key Point: Multi-stage fallback ensures high resolution

5-Stage Resolution Waterfall

Stage 1: Auto-Resolution (EHR + RTE)
Stage 2: Preferred Channel Outreach (SMS/Voice)
Stage 3: Self Check-In at Clinic (Kiosk)
Stage 4: Front Desk with Ambient Listening
Stage 5: Manual/Exception Resolution

Real-Time Eligibility (RTE) Integration

The system calls Real-Time Eligibility at the start of auto-resolution to verify:

  • Insurance status (Active/Inactive)
  • In-Network coverage
  • Copay amounts by service type
  • Deductible remaining

Architecture

This demo shows how the system orchestrates multiple integrations:

• EHR (Epic MPI) for patient identity
• RTE for insurance eligibility
• MyChart for pre-visit confirmation
• SMS/Voice APIs for outreach
• Kiosk for self-service
• Ambient listening for staff interactions
